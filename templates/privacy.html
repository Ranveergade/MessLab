Privacy

Your data in MessLab

MessLab stores your account, meal intents, check-ins, menu feedback and green points. No biometrics, no location tracking, no advertising.

What we keep
  • Your name, email, phone and role, so the mess can identify your account.
  • Meal intents, check-ins and menu feedback, so the kitchen can plan quantities.
  • Green points and badges, which are your own reward history.
  • Short-lived login codes, stored only as a hash, and security events with masked email addresses.
Export or delete

The export is a JSON file you can keep. Deletion removes your account and the rows tied to it; aggregate meal counts remain, without your identifier.
