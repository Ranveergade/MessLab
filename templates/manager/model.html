How the forecast is built
No leakage: every feature is known before the meal starts
1 · Intent signal
Declared yes/maybe intents, intent coverage, historical attendance ratio and the weekly default plan. Intents are stored only for engaged students, so coverage is itself a feature.
2 · Context
Weekday, meal, exam and holiday flags, weather, special menus and lagged participation. Future calendar flags come from DayContext, never from the outcome.
3 · Dish share
A second model predicts each dish's share of meal demand from rolling uptake, category, diet and menu position. Shares are normalised to sum to one before kilograms are computed.
Feedback loop: check-ins and waste logs from closed days become the next training rows. Manager overrides are logged separately for audit; they do not silently rewrite history.
