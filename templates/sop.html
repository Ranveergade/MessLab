Purpose

This SOP establishes a structured and transparent mechanism for planning, finalizing, implementing and monitoring the monthly mess menu. It ensures student participation, variety in food options, clarity regarding item availability and quantities, and timely resolution of reported issues.

2. Composition of the Mess Committee

The Mess Committee comprises four student representatives: two from the Undergraduate (UG) programmes and two from the Postgraduate (PG) programmes. It is the official communication channel between the student community and the mess operator for menu planning, food services and operational discrepancies.

3. Authority and governance
  • The Chairperson – Hostel Residence is the final authority on mess operations, menu approval, policy decisions and dispute resolution.
  • The Mess Committee and the mess operator coordinate directly on routine matters: menu planning, student preferences, menu modifications and operational concerns.
  • Routine operational decisions are ordinarily reached through mutual consultation and agreement.
  • Disagreement, unresolved complaints, repeated deviations from the agreed menu or matters requiring institutional intervention are referred to the Chairperson – Hostel Residence.
  • The Chairperson may review, approve, modify or intervene in any decision concerning mess operations in the institutional and student interest.
4. Monthly menu planning and finalization
Step 1 · Operator submits available items

In the last week of every month the operator gives the committee an exhaustive list of breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, dinner and other items for the forthcoming month, clearly separated into regular service, special preparations and limited-quantity service.

Step 2 · Student consultation

The list is circulated to students. Students send choices and suggestions through the four committee representatives. The committee consolidates preferences, respecting the diversity of UG and PG requirements, and communicates them to the operator.

Step 3 · Mutual discussion and finalization

The committee and operator discuss the proposed menu. On the basis of student preferences, operational feasibility and mutual agreement, the monthly menu is finalized before the month begins and communicated to all students. It is displayed at the canteen notice board.

5. Classification of menu items

The final monthly menu must clearly classify every item. The daily menu and canteen board use these exact categories:

Unlimited

Served without a predefined quantity restriction. The operator ensures adequate preparation throughout service.

Special

Offered for variety. The frequency of each special item is stated in the monthly menu.

Limited

A predefined portion per student. The portion size and monthly frequency are stated, and distribution is equitable.

The final menu must explicitly mention the category and frequency of each item to avoid ambiguity or misunderstanding.

6. Implementation of the finalized menu
  • Once mutually finalized, the monthly menu is the agreed menu for the entire month.
  • The operator makes reasonable arrangements to implement it as communicated.
  • The committee periodically interacts with students and monitors whether the menu is followed.
  • Any deviation is brought to the committee's attention.
7. Changes to the finalized menu
  • Any proposed change by the operator or committee is communicated in advance.
  • The proposed change is discussed between the committee and the operator.
  • Changes are implemented only after mutual agreement.
  • The revised menu or change is communicated to students in advance through the designated channel.
  • In exceptional circumstances (ingredient shortage, unforeseen operational difficulty) the operator immediately informs the committee and proposes a suitable alternative.
  • Unilateral changes to the agreed menu are avoided.
8. Complaints, discrepancies and grievance redressal

Complaints may include non-adherence to the finalized menu, non-availability of agreed items, changes without prior agreement, inadequate portions, shortage of unlimited items, quality, hygiene or food-safety concerns, and any other discrepancy.

1. Report the complaint to any of the four committee representatives.
2. The committee records it and communicates it to the operator.
3. The committee and operator discuss and mutually agree corrective action.
4. The resolution and action taken are recorded by the committee.
5. Unresolved or recurring issues are escalated to the Chairperson – Hostel Residence.
📣 Students should route complaints through the committee rather than starting individual discussions with the operator, so communication stays systematic and accountable.

9. Roles and responsibilities
Mess Committee
  • Collect and consolidate student preferences.
  • Coordinate monthly menu planning.
  • Ensure categories and frequencies are explicit.
  • Communicate the approved menu.
  • Coordinate proposed changes.
  • Record complaints, discrepancies and corrections.
  • Represent UG and PG interests.
Mess operator
  • Submit the exhaustive available-item list.
  • Cooperate in finalizing the monthly menu.
  • Communicate availability and quantities clearly.
  • Implement the mutually agreed menu.
  • Give advance notice of proposed changes.
  • Address complaints and maintain consistency.
Students
  • Participate constructively in preferences.
  • Send suggestions and complaints through representatives.
  • Cooperate with agreed arrangements and guidelines.
10. Monthly review meeting

The committee holds one monthly review meeting with the operator, ideally in the last week of the month alongside menu planning. All four representatives and the operator attend.

Agenda
  1. Review adherence to the current month's approved menu.
  2. Discuss student feedback, complaints and grievances.
  3. Review food quality, quantity, variety and service standards.
  4. Identify recurring discrepancies and corrective measures.
  5. Discuss and mutually finalize the forthcoming menu.

Key discussion points, decisions and agreed actions are documented. Matters needing administrative intervention are referred to the Chairperson – Hostel Residence, the final authority.

11. General guidelines
  • The monthly menu is finalized through mutual consultation and agreement.
  • Transparency and effective communication are maintained between the committee and operator.
  • Student preferences are given due consideration.
  • Neither the operator nor the committee makes unilateral changes to the approved menu.
  • All complaints and discrepancies are addressed through the established committee mechanism.
  • The SOP is reviewed periodically to incorporate suggestions and improve the mess experience.
